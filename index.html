About

I am a PhD student at the Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago. In 2025–2026, I was a Mansueto Urban Doctoral Fellow. My research interests are in urban, public, and health economics.

Before graduate school, I worked as a research assistant on the macroeconomics team at the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago. I hold a B.A. in economics and statistics with a minor in mathematics from the University of California, Davis.
